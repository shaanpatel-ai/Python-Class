The Solar System consists of the Sun and all the objects that move around it.
The Sun is a huge ball of hot gases and gives us light and heat.
There are eight planets in our Solar System.
Mercury is the planet closest to the Sun.
Earth is the third planet from the Sun.
Earth is the only known planet that has life.
The Moon is the natural satellite of Earth.
Planets move around the Sun in fixed paths called orbits.
Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System.
Neptune is the farthest planet from the Sun.
